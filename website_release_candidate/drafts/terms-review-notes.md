# Terms page — review draft notes (not for public display)

The project agreement is a draft and requires owner and US transportation-attorney review. Do not publish a Terms of Service page until the actual business terms and agreement are approved.

Items counsel and owner should confirm before drafting public terms:
- Legal names and contact details of the contracting parties.
- Exact dispatch tasks, carrier authorization, and booking/signature limits.
- Fee basis, invoices, payment timing, pauses, cancellations, and any pass-through expenses.
- Handling of carrier, driver, freight, and broker information.
- Responsibility boundaries, dispute process, governing law, and required disclosures.
- Whether website contact creates any application, consent, or contractual relationship.
