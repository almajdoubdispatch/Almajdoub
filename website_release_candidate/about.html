Behind the Desk

Personal Partnership Over Mass Call Centers.

Almajdoub Dispatch is an independent, remote back-office support service founded and operated by Ahmed Almajdoub. We work directly with a strictly capped circle of US owner-operators who want disciplined execution without losing control of their business.

The Reality of Our Desk

No Pretending to Be a Mega-Brokerage.

Many dispatch operations claim to be sprawling enterprise corporations with hundreds of dispatchers and proprietary AI algorithms. When an owner-operator signs up, they get shuffled between call center agents who don’t know their name, don’t care about their preferred lanes, and push low-paying freight just to make daily quotas.

Almajdoub Dispatch is built on the opposite philosophy: I operate an independent, highly focused desk from Tripoli, Libya, serving the US freight market during core Eastern Standard Time business hours. When you call or message, you reach me directly—the same person who reads your rate confirmations, talks to the brokers, and organizes your paperwork.

Strict Capacity Limit: To protect response time and service depth, our desk is capped at 5 to 7 trucks maximum. When that capacity is reached, intake closes until an existing carrier retires or expands independently.

Regulatory Transparency Notice (49 CFR 371.2)

Almajdoub Dispatch acts as an administrative dispatch support desk and bona fide agent representing motor carriers under written service agreement. We are not a freight broker, freight forwarder, motor carrier, or direct transportation provider. We do not bill shippers, do not take custody of freight, and do not receive or hold freight charges. All contracts for carriage are made directly between the authorized motor carrier and licensed brokers or shippers.
