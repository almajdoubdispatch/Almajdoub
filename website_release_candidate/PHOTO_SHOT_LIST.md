# Photo shot list for a future authentic update

Use original photos only with the operator's consent and rights to publish. Avoid readable plates, third-party logos, rate confirmations, bills of lading, customer names, load details, precise locations, and screens containing personal or business data.

1. **Operator at a real dispatch desk** — natural window light, practical workstation, candid side/rear angle; show a real person only with explicit photo consent. Do not stage a fake call center or claim an unstaffed room is a live operations center.
2. **Carrier reviewing an option while parked** — driver at a safe truck-stop parking spot or inside a parked cab, looking at a phone or paper with all text blurred/unreadable. Never show phone use while driving.
3. **Truck-stop context** — 53-ft Dry Van tractor-trailer parked at a fuel island or service plaza; avoid identifying brands, plates, or other drivers without permission.
4. **Dispatch coordination detail** — hand-written lane notes or a clean screen mock-up with fictional, clearly non-operational values. Do not photograph actual carrier or freight records.
5. **Road and equipment portrait** — real U.S. Class 8 tractor with 53-ft dry van on a safe shoulder/yard or open highway, with location release if the site is recognizable.

For each selected image, save the photographer/owner, source, license or release, permission date, and allowed channels in the project asset record.
