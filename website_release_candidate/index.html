How We Work

The 5-Step Dispatch Sequence

• transparent, carrier-first process where every step is verified before commitment.

1

Find Options

We scan loads aligned strictly with your preferred lanes, gross target, and hours of service (HOS).

2

Review Together

3

Carrier Decides

You have the final say. We only book and execute paperwork when you give explicit verbal or message approval.

4

Follow-Through

We submit driver setup packets, confirm appointments, and assist with broker check calls while you drive safely.

5

Wrap Up & Invoice

You deliver, send us a clean POD photo, and we assist in delivering your billing packet directly to your factoring company.

REGULATORY STATUS & OPERATIONAL BOUNDARIES:
Almajdoub Dispatch is an independent carrier back-office support service operated by Ahmed Almajdoub from Tripoli, Libya, serving US-based interstate motor carriers. Almajdoub Dispatch is not an FMCSA-registered freight broker and does not operate as a motor carrier fleet. We work exclusively under a written Dispatch Authorization Agreement with the motor carrier. All freight transportation agreements (Rate Confirmations) are issued in the carrier’s legal name, and all shipper/broker payments are made directly to the carrier’s account or designated factoring company. The motor carrier retains 100% final authority on all load acceptance decisions.
