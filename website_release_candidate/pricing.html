DISPATCHER - CARRIER SERVICE AGREEMENT & LPOA DRAFT

FMCSA 49 CFR § 371.2 Bona Fide Agent Governance • Ahmed Almajdoub d/b/a Almajdoub Dispatch

1. Strictly Carrier Approval: Carrier retains 100% exclusive authority to accept or decline any load presented. Zero forced dispatch.

2. Direct Broker Monies: Dispatcher does NOT handle, receive, or hold freight charges. All payments flow directly from brokers to Carrier or Carrier's factoring company.

3. Transparent Compensation: Standard: 7.0% Dry Van / 8.0% Reefer (Founding Cohort: 5.0% / 5.5%) Base Linehaul. Invoiced weekly on Friday afternoon; 0% fee on fuel, detention, or TONU.

4. 14-Day Free Trial: $0.00 service fee for first 14 calendar days (up to 1 power unit). Zero long-term commitment.

5. Limited Power of Attorney (LPOA): Strictly for broker packets, insurance certs, and signing Rate Cons only upon prior written consent per load.

Integrity Guarantee

The Zero-Hidden-Fee Standard

We respect the hard-earned dollars of independent truckers. Here is what you will never see on our invoices.

✕ NO Onboarding Fees

We do not charge you to review paperwork, verify insurance certificates, or set up broker profiles. Onboarding is 100% free.

✕ NO Forced Inactivity Fees

If your truck is in the shop or you take two weeks off for family, you are never charged penalty or maintenance retainer fees.

✕ NO Hidden Broker Cuts

We never take kickbacks or broker spreads. The rate confirmation from the broker is the exact rate confirmation you receive and verify.

✕ NO Long Lock-In Contracts

Our agreements operate month-to-month or per-load. If our service is not helping your bottom line, you can cancel with written notice.

✕ NO Handling of Freight Funds

Brokers pay your bank account or factoring company directly. We do not intermediate payments or charge remittance fees.

✓ Clear Weekly Invoices

You receive one clear itemized statement listing each completed load, broker name, gross linehaul, and dispatch fee. Simple and auditable.

Almajdoub Dispatch acts as an administrative dispatch support desk and bona fide agent representing motor carriers under written service agreement. We are not a freight broker, freight forwarder, motor carrier, or direct transportation provider. We do not bill shippers, do not take custody of freight, and do not receive or hold freight charges.
