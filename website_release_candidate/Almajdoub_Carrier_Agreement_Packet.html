1. Scope of Dedicated Back-Office Services

Dispatcher agrees to provide Carrier with dedicated, high-touch administrative dispatch services:

  1. Freight Sourcing & Board Ingestion: Searching electronic freight boards (DAT One, Truckstop, Direct Freight, 123Loadboard) and private broker networks matching Carrier's equipment (strictly 53′ Dry Van or 53′ Refrigerated Reefer) and preferred corridors.
  2. Aggressive Broker Negotiations: Contacting freight brokers, verifying commodity details, piece counts, pickup/delivery windows, and negotiating maximum rates per mile (RPM) and linehaul gross.
  3. Broker Setup & Packet Execution: Submitting Carrier's pre-approved onboarding packets (W-9, COI, Authority certificate, Factoring NOA) to prospective brokers.
  4. Facility Detention Tracking ($65.00/Hour): Monitoring dock milestone timestamps, issuing pre-warnings to brokers at minute 105, and actively pursuing detention billing claims after the standard 2-hour facility grace period.
  5. Telemetry, Geolocation & Weather Safety (Voluntary Consent): To eliminate unpaid deadhead (< 8%), automate detention arrival/departure clocking, calculate home-time proximity, and broadcast live severe weather warnings (I-80 Wyoming high-wind blowovers and mountain chain mandates), Carrier and drivers may voluntarily enable GPS location telemetry via Dispatcher’s carrier application. Tracking is active strictly during on-duty transit, with zero off-duty surveillance.
  6. Electronic Load Board Sub-Seat Authorization: Carrier authorizes Dispatcher (Bona Fide Agent) to access Carrier’s existing freight board subscriptions (including DAT One and Truckstop) via an authorized secondary dispatcher sub-seat under Carrier’s account, or utilize authorized agent credentials strictly for booking Carrier's equipment.

2. Absolute Carrier Approval Gatekeeper (FMCSA Compliance)

3. Transparent Financial Separation & Pricing Structure

  1. Zero Touch of Freight Proceeds: All gross transportation payments flow directly from licensed freight brokers or shippers to Carrier’s designated banking account or factoring company pursuant to Carrier’s Notice of Assignment (NOA). Dispatcher shall never invoice shippers, receive freight checks, or deduct service fees directly from freight revenues.
  2. Approved Dispatch Compensation Models:
    • Model A — Linehaul Percentage Fee (Solo Owner-Operators 1–2 Trucks):
      • Founding Carrier Partner Rate: 5.0% (53′ Dry Van) / 5.5% (53′ Reefer) of gross freight linehaul (grandfathered for continuous active service).
      • Standard Published Rate: 7.0% (53′ Dry Van) / 8.0% (53′ Reefer) of gross freight linehaul.
      • Settlement Schedule: Invoiced weekly on Friday afternoon; strictly due Friday evening (Same-day EOD).
    • Model B — Dedicated Small Fleet Retainer (Multi-Truck Fleets — Subject to Desk Capacity Review):
      • Standard Fleet Retainer: $299.00 USD per active truck / week (Minimum 3 trucks = $897.00 USD/week baseline).
      • Founding Waitlist Fleet Offer: Fleets with 2+ trucks that register on the waiting list and are granted a founding seat pay $245.00 USD per active truck / week (Minimum 2 trucks = $490.00 USD/week baseline). The founding seat and rate continue for the life of that seat, subject to the forfeiture terms below.
      • Advance Payment Terms: Invoiced & prepaid in advance each Monday prior to 07:00 CST before dispatch services commence.
      • Fair Downtime & Breakdown Protection: To qualify for a downtime fee waiver for a non-operating truck, Carrier must provide written notice by Sunday 18:00 CST prior to the Monday billing cycle, accompanied by a certified repair shop work order or tow invoice if downtime exceeds three (3) business days. Minimum fleet floor (2 trucks active) applies to maintain desk reservation.
  3. Excluded Revenues (0.0% Fee): Dispatcher’s fee is calculated strictly on base freight linehaul. Zero service fee (0.0%) applies to fuel surcharges, facility detention reimbursements ($65/hr), layover compensation, or Truck Ordered Not Used (TONU) fees.
  4. Founding Seat Forfeiture: A founding seat remains reserved for its registered waiting-list holder while the account satisfies the applicable payment, minimum-fleet, and active-service requirements. A lapse in continuous service exceeding thirty (30) consecutive days, non-payment, or failure to maintain the minimum fleet floor without mutual written agreement forfeits the founding seat and its $245 rate; the account then returns to the applicable standard terms and the seat may be reassigned.
  5. Grandfathered Founding Rate Protection: Dispatcher covenants that the preferred Founding Partner fee (5.0% Dry Van / 5.5% Reefer) is locked in during continuous active service. A lapse in continuous service exceeding thirty (30) consecutive days without mutual written agreement forfeits founding status, returning Carrier to prevailing standard rates.
  6. Pay-to-Play Weekly Settlement Covenant:
    • Service fee invoices are generated every Friday at 16:00 CST covering completed, clean delivered loads.
    • Invoices are due upon receipt on Friday evening (23:59 CST) with an operational grace period extended to Sunday 23:59 CST.
    • Settlement in full is an express condition precedent to dispatch continuation. Unsettled accounts incur automatic administrative suspension on Monday at 07:00 CST to prevent debt accumulation.
