Privacy & Integrity

Privacy Notice & Data Handling Practices

We believe in honest, minimal data collection. This document explains what information is collected on this website, how it is used, where it travels, and your rights regarding your data.

Operational Context

1. Who Operates This Service

This website is operated by Ahmed Almajdoub, founder of Almajdoub Dispatch, based in Tripoli, Libya, providing independent remote dispatch and back-office administrative support services to commercial motor carriers in the United States.

Direct data inquiries can be addressed to: dispatch@almajdoub.com or telephone +1 (409) 941-7180.

2. Data Flow Architecture & Minimal Collection

We collect strictly the minimum information necessary to assess commercial fit for dispatch services. We do not maintain user logins, do not process online credit card transactions on this site, do not track real-time device GPS locations, and do not request sensitive personal identification numbers (such as SSNs) through public web forms.

3. Tracking Technologies, Cookies & Secure Cloud Data Infrastructure

Zero Advertising Cookies: This website does not place third-party advertising tracking pixels (such as Meta Pixel or LinkedIn Insight) or cross-site behavioral cookies.

Self-Contained Frontend Assets: Visual brand assets, stylesheets, and core scripts are hosted directly on our secure server space without external tracking CDNs.

Secure Cloud Data Infrastructure (Supabase Disclosure): Information submitted through our carrier inquiry and onboarding forms is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and processed through our dedicated enterprise cloud infrastructure hosted with Supabase Inc. (AWS Cloud Infrastructure, SOC2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified). Transmitted information is utilized exclusively for motor carrier qualification, dispatch administration, and regulatory compliance. We strictly do not sell, monetize, or license carrier information to third-party data brokers.

4. WhatsApp & External Messaging Disclosure

If you choose to use our WhatsApp drafting tool:

  • The button drafts a standardized text message and prompts to open the WhatsApp application on your device or WhatsApp Web in your browser.
  • No transmission occurs automatically: You have full visibility into the drafted message and must manually press "Send" in WhatsApp.
  • This website does not log or store the drafted text on a web server when you launch WhatsApp.
  • Once you transmit messages over WhatsApp, communications are governed by WhatsApp LLC’s end-to-end encrypted messaging infrastructure and privacy policy.

5. Communications Standards (TCPA & CAN-SPAM Compliance)

We respect carrier communication preferences:

  • No Automated Robocalls or SMS Blasts: We do not use autodialers, automated robotic calls, or unsolicited mass SMS marketing campaigns.
  • One-on-One Correspondence: Communications from our desk are individual, human responses to your direct inquiry.
  • Zero Selling of Leads: We do not sell, rent, or trade carrier contact information to fuel card companies, insurance agencies, factoring brokers, or freight load boards.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

Inquiry Records: Initial inquiry details that do not result in an active dispatch agreement are deleted or archived after 90 days.

Active Carrier Records: Operational rate confirmations, broker setup packets, and dispatch records for active clients are retained for three (3) years to comply with standard transportation recordkeeping practices and dispute resolution.

Right to Request Deletion: You may request the deletion of your inquiry data at any time by emailing dispatch@almajdoub.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will confirm deletion within five (5) business days.

7. Cross-Border Data Transfer

Because Ahmed Almajdoub operates remotely from Tripoli, Libya, communications and administrative data sent via email, phone, or messaging will be accessed outside the United States for the exclusive purpose of fulfilling dispatch support services. Appropriate organizational safeguards are maintained to protect confidentiality.

8. Updates to This Notice

This notice was last reviewed on October 20, 2026. Any changes to our data handling practices will be posted directly to this URL.

Regulatory Transparency Notice (49 CFR 371.2)

Almajdoub Dispatch acts as an administrative dispatch support desk and bona fide agent representing motor carriers under written service agreement. We are not a freight broker, freight forwarder, motor carrier, or direct transportation provider. We do not bill shippers, do not take custody of freight, and do not receive or hold freight charges. All contracts for carriage are made directly between the authorized motor carrier and licensed brokers or shippers.
